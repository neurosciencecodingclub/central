# Welcome to the Neuroscience Coding Club's Repository
The purpose of the Neuroscience Coding Club is to provide a forum to regularly meet throughout the year to discuss coding approaches related to neuroscience topics across a range of popular programming languages. Our organization serves to bridge the gap between neuroscientists and coders to demystify the intersection of these interdisciplinary fields.

## Links  
Our [Discord](https://discord.gg/WVu9henDqj) is the main method of communication and updates within our club. Anybody is welcome to join our Discord, regardless of student/faculty status!

Our [living document](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/17GkLwX1juq3glL-QsChB8TQ1a9cKruRkzHciwtszgCw/edit?usp=sharing) holds an archive for prior meetings (including those not listed in [meetings](meetings)) and helpful resources.
